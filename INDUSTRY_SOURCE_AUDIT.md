The existing Baostock provider calls `query_stock_industry(date=signal_date)` and maps its returned `industry` to `industry_l2`; `INDUSTRY_SECTIONS` maps the first classification letter to `industry_l1`. There is no permission to substitute a Shenwan/Tushare taxonomy without declaring source drift.
